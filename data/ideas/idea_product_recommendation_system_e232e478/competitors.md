# Competitors Discovered via Google Search

*Live Web Grounded*

Incumbents excel either at catalog scale or single-brand AR, leaving an opening for cross-brand conversational curation.

## IKEA Kreativ

**Founded:** 2022

### Their Idea
Virtual room design and furniture placement using spatial scanning and machine learning.

### Short Bio
IKEA's digital interior design platform developed from its acquisition of Geomagical Labs.

### How We Differ
We aggregate cross-retailer inventory via natural language search rather than locking users into a single brand.

## Collov AI

**Founded:** 2021

### Their Idea
AI-generated interior styling paired with multi-vendor product recommendations.

### Short Bio
A venture-backed startup offering generative interior design tools for consumers and real estate staging.

### How We Differ
We focus on conversational dimension-aware search rather than purely aesthetic generative image re-skinning.

## Google Shopping

**Founded:** 2002

### Their Idea
Universal multi-store e-commerce search aggregation.

### Short Bio
Google's dedicated commercial search platform connecting consumers with distributed retail catalogs.

### How We Differ
We evaluate spatial fit and interactive room context instead of returning disconnected tabular product grids.

## Market landscape

The market bifurcates between closed single-retailer AR tools and broad search engines lacking spatial room context.

## Our positioning

We combine cross-store conversational search with rapid visual placement to bridge product discovery and dimensional fit.
