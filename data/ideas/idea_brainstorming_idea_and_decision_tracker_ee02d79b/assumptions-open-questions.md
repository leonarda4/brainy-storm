# Assumptions and Open Questions

## Assumptions
- Users find playful agent branding appealing enough to switch from existing free-form note-taking tools.
- A free tier or low-cost open-source model can sufficiently power multi-agent workflows.

## Open questions
- Can local or open-source language models replace paid APIs to eliminate operating costs?
- What specific competitors exist in meeting-transcript decision tracking, and how does Brainy Storm differentiate?
