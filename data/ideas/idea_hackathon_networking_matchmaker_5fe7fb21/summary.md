# Hackathon Networking Matchmaker

- ID: `idea_hackathon_networking_matchmaker_5fe7fb21`
- Status: `enriched`

## Summary

An AI-powered matchmaking tool for hackathon participants that uses conversational prompts to extract rich interest profiles and generate contextual conversation starters. The system relies on organizer registration data, simple embeddings, and Postgres to suggest relevant connections.

## Key takeaway

While practical and grounded in real-world utility, the team remains divided on whether the technical implementation is too boring compared to alternative projects.
