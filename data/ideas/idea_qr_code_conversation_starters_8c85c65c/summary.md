# QR Code Conversation Starters

- ID: `idea_qr_code_conversation_starters_8c85c65c`
- Status: `enriched`

## Summary

Users struggling with networking conversations can scan someone's QR code to receive tailored conversation starters. The group debated whether this concept should be a standalone product or just a feature, with some suggesting it could serve as an MVP.

## Key takeaway

The team has not reached a consensus on whether the QR code concept should be a standalone product or a feature within a larger networking tool.
