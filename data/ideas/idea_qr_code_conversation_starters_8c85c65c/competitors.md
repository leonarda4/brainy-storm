# Competitors Discovered via Google Search

*Live Web Grounded*

Existing tools separately address contact sharing, card-based prompt games, and dating profile prompts, leaving room for personalized in-person icebreakers.

## Popl

**Founded:** 2019

### Their Idea
Contactless digital networking profile sharing via smart hardware and QR codes.

### Short Bio
Popl builds NFC and QR-based digital business cards to streamline professional in-person networking.

### How We Differ
We deliver personalized conversational icebreakers to stimulate immediate dialogue rather than transmitting static contact info.

## We're Not Really Strangers

**Founded:** 2018

### Their Idea
Prompt-based card game centered on deepening interpersonal connections through targeted questions.

### Short Bio
WNRS produces card games and digital tools with prompt-based questions to deepen conversations.

### How We Differ
Our questions are uniquely tailored to a scanned person's specific interests rather than drawn from standardized decks.

## Hinge

**Founded:** 2012

### Their Idea
Profile-bound text prompts that serve as conversational hooks for messaging.

### Short Bio
Hinge is a relationship-focused dating platform utilizing user-answered prompts to inspire organic chats.

### How We Differ
We enable immediate, real-world social engagement across diverse events through on-demand physical QR scans.

## Market landscape

The market is split between static digital business cards, generic conversational card games, and prompt-driven digital dating apps.

## Our positioning
