# Assumptions and Open Questions

## Assumptions
- Event attendees are willing to present and scan QR codes before initiating spontaneous face-to-face discussions.
- Tailored prompts provide noticeably superior conversational outcomes compared to browsing a standard professional profile.

## Open questions
- What specific profile data points will be used to generate relevant, contextual conversation starters?
- Should the capability be tested as a standalone pilot app or integrated into a comprehensive event networking platform?
